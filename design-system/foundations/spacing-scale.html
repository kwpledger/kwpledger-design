Spacing

Seven steps derived from a 4px base. Expressed in rem, so the whole scale responds to the reader's browser font size rather than overriding it.

Pick a step; never type a pixel value. The scale is coarse on purpose — seven options force a decision about hierarchy instead of nudging by twos. If nothing fits, the layout is usually the problem, not the scale.

The steps

--space-2xs0.5rem · 8px
--space-xs0.75rem · 12px

--space-m1.5rem · 24px
--space-l2.5rem · 40px
--space-xl4rem · 64px
--space-2xl6rem · 96px

Width limits

Two, and the second is exactly twice the first.

--measure 34rem — body copy
--page 68rem — full-width layout

Rhythm in practice

Related things share the smaller step; a boundary between ideas takes the larger one. Proximity is the grouping signal — a heading close to its paragraph and far from the previous one needs no rule, box, or color to read as attached.
