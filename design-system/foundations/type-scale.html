The pairing settled for both YouTube channels, which is what makes every surface read as the same family. Latin subset only, ~56KB total — self-hosted, so no third-party request and no dependency on an external host staying up.

Lora ships at weight 600 only. It is a static face; Hanken Grotesk is variable (100–900). Ask Lora for any other weight and the browser fakes it — a synthesized bold that does not match the real one. That is what --fw-display exists to prevent: use var(--fw-display), never a literal.

The two faces

Lora — the display face
--font-display · --fw-display: 600

Headings, pull quotes, the signature moments. Serif, with enough contrast to carry a large canvas.

Hanken Grotesk — the body face
--font-body · variable 100–900

Everything else. Any weight in range is real, not synthesized.

Measure

--measure is 34rem and --page is 68rem — exactly double. Body copy sits at the measure; full-width layout sits at the page.

This paragraph is capped at var(--measure). Line length is a legibility constraint, not a stylistic preference — past roughly 75 characters the eye loses its place returning to the next line, and the reader's comprehension drops before they notice anything is wrong.

Fonts are reached relatively here. In production tokens/fonts.css points at /fonts/ absolutely — copy the fonts/ directory to your host's served root, OFL-NOTICE.txt included. The license travels with the files.
