Written is not deployed

Your AI policy travels seven layers before it changes anything

Most of the AI policies I’ve read are specific through layer three.
Layer 1

Principle

The value nobody argues with: “use AI responsibly.” Everyone has one.

Layer 2

Policy

The written rule. Approved tools, prohibited data, named owners.

Layer 3

Standard

What “verify the output” specifically requires, in writing.

Layer 4

Workflow

Where that step actually sits in the real work, and who owns it.

Layer 5

Tool behavior

What the software permits, blocks, or quietly logs by default.

Layer 6

User action

What one person actually does at 4:00 on Thursday, under pressure.

Layer 7

Evidence

What remains afterward proving it happened — and what it should change.
