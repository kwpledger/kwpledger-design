Little AI vs. Big AI

Two kinds of AI, and one difference that decides who is accountable for the output.
Type A

Little AI

Rules-based and deterministic

  • Follows predefined rules and conditions
  • Produces predictable, repeatable results
Same input Same output, every time
Examples
  • A phone tree routing calls by the button pressed
  • A Power Automate flow triggering actions on conditions
Type B

Big AI

Generative and probabilistic

  • Produces output from learned patterns, not fixed rules
  • Handles ambiguous or open-ended requests
Same input Potentially a different output
Examples
  • Claude answering a question in natural language
  • An AI synthesizing documents into a first draft
Why it matters

Little AI executes decisions already encoded in the system. Big AI generates new output — so people must judge, verify, and remain accountable for it.
