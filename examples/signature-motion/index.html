Motion study · short mark, ringed

The signature, written the way it was written

Nothing here is redrawn. What you see is the signature file itself, traced from a Sharpie on a 3×5 card in 2013. Each point of ink is uncovered at the moment the pen first reached it, worked out from the path the pen took, so no stroke can appear before the pen gets there. When the pen finishes, the page shows the original file and nothing else.

The order is yours. The first stroke runs top to bottom, the pen doubles back over the two short peaks, goes down the right side of the descender loop and back up the left, then climbs the stem and closes on the bowl. Then the ring draws clockwise, starting from the upper left where the signature began.

Reduced motion. If your device asks for less motion, the mark appears complete and does not animate. The light and dark pages use two different files, the black glyph and the inverted one, rather than one file recolored.
