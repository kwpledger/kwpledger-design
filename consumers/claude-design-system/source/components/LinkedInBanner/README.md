# LinkedInBanner

The 1584×396 (4:1) LinkedIn profile banner, dark register, in two modes: event (eyebrow, title, subtitle, date row) and standing (the positioning line).

- The left 528px carries no content: the profile photo covers it.
- Title at most 9 words, subtitle at most 12 and one line. The eyebrow is the only accent.
- The mark is `logo_long_inv.svg` at 96px tall, with no ring.
- Switch back to the standing mode once an event is over.
