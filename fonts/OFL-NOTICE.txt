All four files are the latin subset only (~149 KB combined; the two italics
load only when a page uses them). Characters outside
that range fall back to the next family in the stack, which is the intended
behavior — see the unicode-range declarations in tokens/fonts.css.

Why self-hosted rather than loaded from Google Fonts: no third-party request
on page load, and no dependency on an external host remaining available.

To replace either typeface, drop in a new .woff2, update the matching
@font-face block in tokens/fonts.css and the --font-display / --font-body
tokens in tokens/base.css, and check --fw-display still falls inside the new
face's weight range.
